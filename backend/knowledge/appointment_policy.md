# Appointment Policy

- **Rescheduling fee:** ₹300 — applies only if the change is made within 24 hours of the scheduled
  appointment time.
- **Cancellation fee:** ₹500 — applies only if the cancellation is made within 24 hours of the
  scheduled appointment time.
- Outside that 24-hour window, rescheduling and cancellation are free.
- A booking always requires the patient's full name — appointments are never made anonymously, even
  for a phone number already on file.
- Same-day appointments respect a buffer between consecutive appointments at each branch, so back-to-
  back double-bookings never happen.

**Never state a fee to the caller unless the reschedule/cancel tool response actually includes one.**
The tool applies this policy automatically — do not calculate or guess the fee yourself.
