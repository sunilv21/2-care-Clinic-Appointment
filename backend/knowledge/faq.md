# Frequently Asked Questions

**What should I bring to my first appointment?**
A valid photo ID and any prior medical records or prescriptions relevant to the visit.

**Can I book for a family member?**
Yes — the caller should give the patient's own full name during booking, even if calling on their
behalf. If the phone number is a shared/family line, the agent will confirm which family member the
appointment is for.

**How early should I arrive?**
About 10 minutes before the scheduled time.

**Do you see walk-ins?**
The clinic prioritizes scheduled appointments; call to check same-day availability rather than
assuming a walk-in slot is open — always check via `find_availability` / `get_earliest_slot`.

For anything not covered here, or a clinical question, log a follow-up rather than guessing.
